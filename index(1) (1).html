Understand the building blocks

Each asset class can play a different role. The right mix depends on goals, horizon, risk capacity, liquidity and suitability.

Equity

Growth-oriented asset class with higher market volatility. Typically more relevant to longer horizons.

Fixed income / FD

Can provide stability, predictable interest or income characteristics, subject to product terms and issuer risk.

Gold

Diversification asset whose price can fluctuate and may behave differently from equities.

REITs / InvITs

Listed real-asset exposure with market-linked prices and distributions; risks include interest rates and property/business conditions.
